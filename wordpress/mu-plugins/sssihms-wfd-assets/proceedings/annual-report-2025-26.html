03
Holistic patient care

Counselling, nutrition and rehabilitation

SAI Counselling

22,239

patients counselled (13,301 cardiac · 8,938 neurosurgery)

Since 2001 the SAI (Spirituality Awareness Integration) Counselling Department has offered emotional, psychological and spiritual support to cardiac and neurosurgery patients and their caregivers — on the day of admission, the day of surgery and the day of discharge, with bedside counselling as needed. The department works Monday to Saturday with at least two dedicated volunteers each day.

Clinical nutrition

90,000

patient meals prepared and served at no charge

  • 7,974 breakfasts, lunches and dinners for attendants of paediatric and severely ill patients
  • 33,246 clear-liquid and liquid feeds for post-surgical patients and those unable to chew
  • 900 customised high-protein enteral formula feeds for long-stay and ventilated patients

Medical nutrition therapy includes sodium- and fluid-restricted plans for advanced heart failure and post-CABG patients, and texture-modified diets for neuro patients.

Physiotherapy & rehabilitation

24,197

inpatient therapy sessions

The Department of Physiotherapy provides inpatient rehabilitation across super-speciality departments: intensive care and post-operative management, cardiopulmonary rehabilitation, neurological and orthopaedic rehabilitation, electrotherapy, exercise therapy, hydro-thermal care (wax and cryotherapy) and scar management.

A new rehabilitation facility opened on 06-Dec-2025 (see Equipment).

Campus & facilities

Infrastructure

₹2 crore

invested by Sri Sathya Sai Central Trust in the upgrade and maintenance of infrastructure, including furniture, engineering and IT.

Murti Sthāpana of Lord Ganapati

An idol of Lord Ganapati in a standing posture was enshrined in an ornate mandapa at the main entrance. On the terrace, in the former place of the Ganesha idol, a photograph of Bhagawan has been installed.

Hospital façade lighting

As an offering for Bhagawan’s Centenary, and given the hospital’s location about 200 metres from the metro station, the façade is now illuminated nightly with pillar highlight lighting and full window and dome lighting, chosen for aesthetics, energy efficiency and ease of maintenance.

Water conservation

1,691 water-saving aerators were fitted to all taps in the hospital and residential complex with the support of FluxGen, and about 120 percolation wells were built across the campus with United Way Bengaluru to recharge groundwater.

Patient attender accommodation

With the support of the Sattva Group, the GD Salarpuria attender block was upgraded: bathrooms and toilets on all three floors, 204 cots with mattresses and pillows, new curtains, paver pathways, and corridor and façade painting. Public toilets near the registration block were also upgraded.

HVAC — chiller replacement

A Bluestar 220 TR water-cooled screw chiller using R-134a refrigerant replaced one of three YORK Millennium chillers installed in 2000, which used the now phased-out R-22 refrigerant.

Electrical & patient logistics

The main 11 kV vacuum circuit breakers were replaced for power reliability and safety. A 14-seater electric buggy now carries elderly and mobility-impaired patients across campus.

  • 31-Jul-2025 — mammography screening camp for 40 staff members with the Poornasudha Cancer Foundation.
  • 30-Oct-2025 — breast cancer awareness and clinical training by Dr. Jayanthi for 13 healthcare professionals, who now educate patients and facilitate screening registrations.

18–19-Aug-2025
Centenary

Sri Sathya Sai Prema Pravahini at SSSIHMS

The Prema Pravahini Ratham was received with Vedam, 108 Mahilas carrying Kalashams, Nadaswaram and bhajans, while nursing and allied health students showered flowers along the pathway. The evening programme “Sarvam Sai Mayam” included a puppet show on Bhagawan’s childhood, devotional songs by the Sai Sharanam group, a Balvikas drama on the five human values, and an offering of 100 dishes. The next morning began with Suprabhatam, Nagarsankeertan and Paduka Puja before the Ratham continued its journey.

28-Aug-2025
SSSGH Whitefield

49th anniversary of Sri Sathya Sai General Hospital

Marking the start of its Golden Jubilee year, staff and families gathered at Trayee Brindavan. The Director felicitated founding cardiologist Dr. Barooah and senior physician Dr. Ramkumar for decades of voluntary service.

24-Sep-2025
Curtain raiser 1 · Facility Management Services

“Oneness — The Eternal Legacy: 100 Years of Love and 25 Years of Healing”

A drama by FMS staff, woven with three recorded bhajans sung by Bhagawan, depicting the hospital’s 25-year journey on the ideals of “Love All, Serve All” and “Help Ever, Hurt Never”.

18-Oct-2025
Curtain raiser 2 · College of Nursing

“Anugrahaya Kṛtajnata” — Gratitude for Grace

B.Sc Nursing students presented the skit “Karuna Mahi”, the dance drama “Jeevanasya Tridhare”, a Burrakatha on Namasmarana, a Silver Jubilee quiz, the retrospective film “Saga of Endless Memories”, and the closing light show “Arogyaadhipah”.

25-Oct-2025
Curtain raiser 3 · Doctors & nurses

A Festival Walk with Swami

A two-hour guided campus walk conceived as a Gram Mahotsava, following a new DNB resident through festivals of many faiths — Sankranti, Shivaratri, Ram Navami, Onam, Dussehra, Deepavali, Ramadan, Id-ul-Fitr and Christmas — with Vedam, Abhangs, carols, instrumental music by clinicians and a Nurses’ Day skit.

14-Nov-2025
Public outreach

Commemorative inflatable display

A 30-foot inflatable bearing Bhagawan’s image was installed atop the hospital to mark the Centenary, the Silver Jubilee of SSSIHMS Whitefield and the Golden Jubilee of Sri Sathya Sai General Hospital — visible round the clock across the Whitefield–EPIP area and to metro commuters.

30-Nov-2025
Karthika month

Deepotsavam 2025 — “25 Years of Healing with Compassion”

Doctors, nurses, technologists and support staff lit 5,000 lamps at the Central Dome to form a floor mandala with Bhagawan’s Centenary logo, the hospital silhouette, the SSSIHMS crest and the Silver Jubilee inscription, flanked by ten floral lamp arrangements representing the hospital’s stakeholders.

06-Dec-2025
Preventive cardiology

Launch of SAI SPARSH

Sri R.J. Rathnakar, Managing Trustee, launched the Sri Sathya Sai Preventive Cardiology and Rehabilitation Programme with Dr. Nayanjeet Chaudhury, Dr. Reeta Varyani and Dr. Prayaag Kini. After volunteer training the programme became operational on 19-Jan-2026.

06-Dec-2025
Volunteers

Seva Vandanam

Over 650 volunteers attended in person — sevadals, visiting clinicians, counsellors, technical teams, corporate volunteers and blood donors. Sharings included Dr. Sivaraman Yegyaraman (USA), who built the EP programme over 45 service visits; Dr. Manjunath K.S., who performed the hospital’s first hip and knee replacements; Smt. Anusuya Kodandaram, who led women sevadals for 25 years; Sri Gunaranjan P.S. (United Care), whose 200+ alumni handle 1,400 telehealth calls a day; and Sri Seshasayee A.N. (Liquid Love), marking 40,000 blood donors over 25 years. Doctors and staff served dinner prasadam to all volunteers.

02–03-Jan-2026
Silver Jubilee

25-hour Akhanda Bhajan

From 16:00 hrs on 02-Jan-2026 to 17:00 hrs on 03-Jan-2026 at Dhanvantari Hall, with department-wise teams of staff, residents, nursing students, alumni, Samithi members and well-wishers.

18–19-Jan-2026
Silver Jubilee · Prasanthi Nilayam

Parthi Yatra and Silver Jubilee observance

A large contingent of staff, students, patients and sevadals offered gratitude at Prasanthi Nilayam. The first day included Aumkaram, Suprabhatam, Nagarasankeertan, a visit to Chaitanya Jyothi and the Planetarium, and a photo session at the Sannidhi.

On the anniversary, 19-Jan-2026, the morning session at Sai Kulwant Hall featured Vedam by the lady staff and talks on preventive cardiac health by Dr. Reeta Varyani and Dr. Nayanjeet Chaudhury. Trustees released The Heart of Health, the SAI SPARSH counsellor-coach guide, and ManoHriday, the Silver Jubilee souvenir; SPARSH Level-2 volunteers received certificates. The Managing Trustee held a satsang for all yatris. In the evening, the Director presented the Annual Report before a cultural presentation, Bhagawan’s Divine Discourse and Arathi.

24-Jan-2026
Silver Jubilee · Whitefield

Silver Jubilee celebration on the hospital lawns

Trustees, staff, students, alumni, former staff, Balvikas children, patients and well-wishers gathered for Vedam, the lighting of the lamp and cake cutting. The Director presented the Annual Report; Trustees Padma Shri Dr. V. Mohan and Sri S.S. Naganand addressed the gathering, and the ManoHriday newsletter was released. A cultural programme by staff, nursing and allied health students and Balvikas children followed, then Divine Discourse, bhajans and Mahamangala Arathi. In the afternoon, departments hosted “Department Reconnect” sessions with former colleagues and alumni, closing with “Chai pe Charcha”.
