EDITORIAL

In this issue’s editorial, we are sharing with you the happenings over the past 25 years in the Counselling Department. We are also projecting the scenario for the coming 25 years. We have included patient stories to highlight the fact that Faith in God is innate in human beings, and this faith factor will always prevail as long as life threatening illnesses prevail.

The 25th Anniversary of our hospital is not merely a milestone reached, but truly an epochal time for the Counselling Department, which will hold a place of pride in the annals of this venerable institution. We had the privilege of being part of this unique SAI Counselling program, conceived in Swami’s Divine Wisdom, with two of us being chosen by Swami as His instruments from the inception of the Hospital in 2001 until 2011. When He passed away, there were twenty-two trained SAI Counsellors, virtually all who had the good fortune of having interacted with Him. In His inimitable way, Swami inspired us to write a handbook on the ‘Selection and Training of SAI Counsellors’, anticipating its need.

Looking ahead at the next 25 years to be able to celebrate an equivalent 50th Anniversary, there will be several challenges. To maintain the quality of service provided to the patients, we need to continue the present model of personal/tele-counselling. Screening standards for our applicants will be very strict with zero tolerance to meet Swami’s standards. Based on age and health issues, we will lose around 30% of our present staff in the next 10 years. This trend may continue. It is extremely unlikely that AI/Robotic technologies will enter our space in the next 25 years. However AI can simplify our data gathering and records maintenance.

Swami has left behind a sacred and priceless legacy for us to use and maintain in its original form without making any fundamental changes.

Jai Sai Ram........................................................................................................................................................................................ The Editorial Team (Contact person: Umesh Rao <umesh.r@sssihms.org.in>)

Swami’s Divine Message

Every man should recognise that the body has been given to him to render service to others. You must use the body for promoting the welfare of society. Of what use is the endless study of books if you do not use your knowledge for the good of others? A mind that is not utilised for imparting joy to others or a body that is not used for the service of others is totally useless. The way to love God is to love all and serve all.

Keep faith in God, steady like life-breath. Man forgets his true nature because of attachment and egoism. Man should develop firm faith in God. Faith should be like your life-breath, which goes on inhaling and exhaling at all times without respite. Respiration goes on, whatever work you may be doing and whatever be the state of your mind. Likewise, in all situations and at all times, your faith in God should never leave you. It should not change according to whether your desires are fulfilled or not. Like your lifebreath, your faith should remain steady, through joy and sorrow, loss and gain, pain and pleasure. Treat whatever happens as good for you. Have that sense of fortitude.

Dedicate all your actions to God. Realise that there is no greater quality than truth. Truth is God. Dharma is His ornament. Adhere to these two as the highest attributes of humanness. Plant the seed of love in your pure hearts. From that tree of love, you will have the fruits of forbearance, compassion and kindness. Always be smiling. Face every situation in life, including death, with a smile. Speeches would be immensely effective if the words came not merely from the lips but from the heart. There should be complete harmony in thought, word and deed. I desire that you should all redeem your lives by contemplating on God, cherishing good thoughts and engaging yourselves in the service of your fellow men. That is your Duty, and Duty is God.

Swami’s Discourse at the Institute Auditorium on 19-1-1997.

PATIENT ALOKE KUMAR DUTTA’S STORY: ‘HEADING BACK TO NORMALCY AND A WISH FULFILLED’

“Engage yourselves in service activity. Consider social service as service to God. This is the best way to earn God’s Love. Love all and serve all. Your entire life will be sanctified thereby”.

  • Sathya Sai Baba

Patient Aloke Kumar Datta, aged 53 years, a retired Shashastra Seema Bal (Para-Military Force) Inspector, came to our Hospital for his follow-up checkup in April 2025. After the checkup the doctors told him that the pacemaker is in a good condition and functioning well. He told the Counsellor that, “he is Swami’s blessed child, He is taking care of him”.

Patient Aloke Kumar Dutta hails from North 24 Paraganas District of WB. He was suffering from multiple episodes of sudden fainting spells for short durations in the past year and came to our Hospital seeking medical treatment for his illness, on 1st April 2024. His wife Mou Dutta accompanied him as caretaker; they have a twenty one year old son. They are a spiritual family, their family deities being Bholenath and Ma Kali.

He was advised he needed a Permanent Pacemaker Implantation (PPI).The procedure was done on 2nd April 2024 successfully and he was discharged on

th

April 2024 and was asked to come for a check up on 12th April 2024. After that he was told it would only be a routine annual check up.

Patient Aloke Kumar Dutta when he came in April 2025 for his routine annual check up, he shared with the Counsellor his journey back to normalcy, after getting treated successfully for Arrhythmia. He recalls and reminisces the reassurance and support given by the Doctors of SSSIHMS, when they said that he would be able to lead a normal life after the pacemaker is implanted -he felt so relieved.

True to the words of the Doctor and his disciplined life-style he follows from SSB training, Patient Aloke Kumar was able to recoup and get back to his normalcy in a span of 15 to 20 days post the PPI procedure. Sharing his routine, Patient Aloke Kumar says that his day starts at 5 a.m. with morning walk and yogasana. He also does his breathing exercises. He walks for 40 minutes without any difficulty; he can carry weights up to 10 kgs and has no difficulty in riding his bike. He did not feel any discomfort as such. He is an inspiration, especially to the youth in his town. They regularly join him in his morning routine and exercise. Patient Aloke Kumar, says that prior to treatment he could not climb 20 steps and would also fall unconscious. He also had difficulty breathing. At present, after the treatment, he is able to climb 2 floors comfortably at his home.

Patient Aloke Kumar, in one of the conversations shared with the counsellor expressed his wish to serve and do seva in Swami’s Organisation, as a token of Gratitude for the second Life bestowed on him by Swami. True to his yearning, he has joined the Sri Sathya Sai Seva Organisation in Barackpur, WB. He takes part in various Seva activities of the samithi like cleaning the premises, attending bhajans and taking part in the prasadam distribution. He along with other sevadal members distributed food packets in the Vriddhashram and orphanage. He was thrilled to share that, as part of Swami’s Centenary celebrations, he was blessed to take part in the Sai Run (Run for Unity). He participated in the walking category and successfully completed the walk. Even now he takes part in 5 Km to 8 Km walkathons. Patient Aloke Kumar is also a member of an NGO in which there are about 150 volunteers. They donate food, clothes to the poor and also sponsor education for poor children. They also organize blood donors for needy patients.

As Patient Aloke Kumar has retired from Service he and his wife Mou spend their time in a fruitful way. Mou is an affectionate and an emotional person. Mou has great belief in Maa Kali, and Patient Aloke Kumar has firm faith in Bholenath and Sathya Sai Baba. He supports Mou’s favorite hobby of gardening, which is very therapeutic for her. They have a beautiful garden which brings happiness and peace. Patient AlokeKumar experienced a lot of peace and felt relaxed when BRMC(Body Relaxation and Mind Cleansing) was taught in our Hospital. He says “All his fears disappeared”. He feels that the Counseling support provided here is a very unique and essential part of Healing.”

Patient Aloke Kumar and his wife Mou reverentially offer their gratitude to their Beloved Mother Sai. What they experienced cannot be expressed in words. On entering the premises of the hospital, he was awestruck by the beautiful garden. He was humbled by seeing the cooperation and helpful nature of the sevadals, staff and other attenders. He is especially grateful to the Doctors, as they motivated him to go in for the procedure and clearly explained the benefit of having the Pacemaker. He really liked the quietude - the “Nishabda”, the cleanliness, the discipline and friendliness of all the doctors, nurses and staff in our Hospital.

To Patient Aloke Kumar Dutta, Swami’s Hospital is verily a Mandir - a ‘Temple of Healing’, which has taught him Life Lessons and has given him a new lease of Life. As he says he has realized the true meaning of “Service to Humanity”.

PATIENT MARFAT KHAN’S STORY: ‘FAITH IN ALLAH AND A POSITIVE ATTITUDE TO LIFE’

Marfat Khan is 39 years old and is from Howrah, West Bengal, and has been working in Kerala doing embroidery work for the past 8 years. His wife also works and does embroidery work. He has two daughters, 10-year-old Masumulla and 8-year-old Muquadittar. The family lives in Midnapore, West Bengal. His caretaker was his brother Saddam, who lives in Kolkata and works there.

In 2023, when he was at work, he had frequent spells of breathlessness and once he fainted. He was then admitted to a hospital in Kerala itself, and he believes that he was in a very serious condition, and Allah helped him to recover. Thereafter, he was on medication, but he continued to be serious, and his Doctor advised him that he needed surgery and to seek urgent treatment elsewhere.

His first visit to SSSIHMS was on 7th April 2025, and he visited alone for a checkup. He was told that his condition needed immediate surgery as his valve was bad, and was advised to bring his relative immediately. His brother Saddam came immediately as his caregiver. He was apprised that his condition was risky. He was shattered, lost all hope and surrendered to Allah. At this juncture, the Hospital requested counselling for Marfat. A senior counsellor from our Counselling Department counselled him the night before surgery and filled him with hope and confidence, and trust in his God, Allah. He felt reassured that he was not alone and that all were with him. He was operated successfully for a Mitral Valve Replacement and discharged on 14th April 2025.

During discharge counselling, he expressed that he had slight pain, but breathlessness was gone. He was advised a diet to be followed strictly. BRMC was done for him and his brother, Saddam, and both of them felt relaxed. He was so used to working from a very young age of 5 years, when his father passed away, he had no time for anything else. He prays and reads the Quran daily and keeps himself positive. He is not educated.

Post-discharge, he has followed the routine as advised by our Doctors and practices the BRMC, taught by our Counsellor and spends time with his children. Marfat is following a very disciplined life, post-surgery, following a strict diet as advised by the dietician, without greens and non-vegetarian food once a month. He has also been advised not to lift any heavy weights.

He is contacting the Hospital and the Doctors when needed. He had come for a check-up 2 months ago, and the Doctor had told him to maintain himself and continue with the blood test every month and monitor the same. The Doctor has asked him to continue with his work /profession so that he will know his abilities and limits. His next check-up at our Hospital is in July 2026.

He is feeling much better and is very positive. He feels difficulty in breathing when there is smoke, especially now that he has to cook for himself. He has realised this and will look into the possibility of bringing his wife for a few days, and also to consult our Hospital Doctor.

His appreciation for the Hospital and staff and their dedication is very genuine and from the bottom of his heart.

PATIENT PROSENJIT PAUL'S STORY: ‘GOD’S GRACE WITH COMPASSION’

Patient Prosenjit Paul, aged 29 years, accompanied by his father Nirapada Paul, was admitted to the Neurosurgery Department at SSSIHMS on 28th August 2021. Immediately upon admission, the counsellor reached out to both Prosenjit and his father over the phone and began supportive counselling. Both were overwhelmed with fear, stress, and anxiety about the diagnosis and forthcoming treatment. Through gentle counseling and introduction to BRMC therapy (Body Relaxation and Mind Cleansing), both father and son began to feel a sense of calm and gradually shared details about their journey.

Prosenjit lives in Dumdum, Kolkata, with his parents and younger brother, and managed a small bookshop. Until four months ago, life was normal, but then he began experiencing double vision, progressing to blurred vision, making reading impossible. Despite numerous tests at a local hospital, the diagnosis remained unclear until an MRI revealed a brain tumor.

He suffered from headaches and seizures, which intensified with time, bringing great distress to Prosenjit and his family. Upon learning about SSSIHMS through Swami’s Grace, they decided to seek treatment at SSSIHMS Whitefield. At SSSIHMS, Prosenjit was diagnosed with Left Frontal Anaplastic Astrocytoma (Grade 3), a malignant brain tumour. On 29th August 2021, the neurosurgeons successfully performed his surgery. Post-operatively, Prosenjit showed steady improvement and was discharged in stable condition on 6th September 2021. Throughout this time, both Prosenjit and his father expressed deep gratitude for Swami’s Blessings and the compassionate care they received from the hospital staff. Their faith offered much-needed strength during a difficult time.

On 9th September, while traveling back home to Kolkata by train, Prosenjit experienced recurrent seizures. His father, initially unsure and frightened, sought help from fellow passengers to get off at Katpadi Junction. Through divine providence, they arranged an ambulance and were rushed to SSSIHMS Whitefield emergency department. Prosenjit was treated for disorientation, fever, and infected bed sores and showed remarkable recovery. Soon after, he was discharged on 16th September 2021.

Prosenjit’s father diligently followed all medical advice to ensure his son's continued wellness. Prosenjit underwent 31 sessions of radiation and five rounds of chemotherapy postoperatively as per the doctor's advice. Regular follow-ups at SSSIHMS revealed encouraging progress. Financial hardship led to the family selling their bookstore. A ray of hope was regained when his vision improved. He felt encouraged to apply for a job, and his perseverance helped him get a job at the very clinic of the ophthalmologist who initially treated him. His resilience and return to work have been a source of hope and joy to the family.

Spiritually, Prosenjit feels a deep connection to Swami's Grace. During his stay at our hospital, he would often visit the shrine at our Counselling Department to pray and receive Swami’s prasad of holy ash (Vibhuthi), which reassured him. He is currently filled with positive vibrations and feels blessed despite life's challenges. Prayer has been a source of comfort and motivation in his healing journey.

The counselling team continues to support Prosenjit and his family with prayers and emotional guidance, hoping for his complete recovery and a fulfilling life ahead.

His story is a testament to courage, faith, and the healing power of compassionate care, and above all, God’s Grace.

PATIENT RAJENDRAN’S STORY: ‘PATIENT’S FAITH IN GOD AND SWAMI’S MIRACULOUS HELP TO THE PATIENT’S WIFE’

Patient Rajendran R, aged 58 years, was working as a security guard in a hospital in Coimbatore, TN. He has total faith in Lord Shiva and prays to Him twice everyday. His first wife passed away in November 2018 due to cancer. They did not have a child of their own but, Rajendran had adopted a baby boy at a very early age and had educated him to become a dentist and had also got him married. His first wife was alive to see a baby boy born to their adopted son. Soon after, within a month's time she passed away. Two years later Rajendran married Devaki, a very pious and loving person.

He had to take a loan of 15 lakhs for his first wife's treatment from a lady close to his home but was not regular in paying the interest or the capital, so he was bullied into signing his own property worth 1 crore to this lady. He was given a compensation of 15 lakhs. Since memories were very strong, he moved to another house after paying 5 lakhs to that house owner.

Rajendran’s stress level became very high and he fainted one day while on duty. He was immediately rushed to a nearby private hospital in an unconscious state. He was diagnosed with three blocks in the heart. They could not afford the treatment cost so his wife, Devaki took him to a Govt. hospital. He regained his consciousness in the ICU there. He was asked to undergo Angio there but since it was corona time in 2021, he was discharged and the hospital told him that they will call him back in 2 month's time. After returning home Devaki made a homemade medicine that she had heard about, for him to drink first thing in the morning. He returned to the Govt. hospital after two months; his CAG was clear and he was discharged without treatment.

He had been finding it very difficult to walk due to breathlessness for the past few months. They were regulars at the nearby Swami’s Samithi; so the convenor of that Samithi sent them to our Hospital for treatment. Patient Rajendran was admitted to our Hospital on 16/12/2025 and a CAG was done on 19/12/2025. He was discharged on 20/12/2025 under medical management. He has been a diabetic, type II, for the past four years and is on medication. He is an ex-smoker and is overweight, 71 kg. He was advised that he needs to take care of himself.

His caregiver Devaki is a highly devout person with faith in Ambal. Rajendran was admitted to Swami's hospital to undergo ICD. On the day of his procedure Devaki had to go to the ward very early in the morning when it was still dark, from where all the caregivers stay. She was afraid of the dogs, and being new to the place she wanted somebody to accompany her but there was no one at that time. She stood in front of Swami’s photo and prayed to Him. Immediately a person with a blanket on his arm came down the stairs close to the picture.

Devaki looked at him expectantly and he nodded his head silently and motioned her to come with him. On the way he had walked ahead and waved to her to follow. They went together up the emergency ramp and when she reached the staircase going up to the cardiac ward her escort had simply vanished. She looked at the lift but it was on the ground floor only. Our most benevolent Swami had rushed to escort His devotee in her time of need. One recalls the words of Shirdi Sai Baba:

One Call Of Love And I Shall Answer, One Honest Prayer And I Shall Fulfil, I Reside Where There Is Complete Faith And Devotion.

Devaki's blessings extended beyond this when her husband was discharged with just CAG and the doctor told her that he does not require ICD.

They both returned back to Coimbatore on the same day and they both decided not to look back and feel sad about the past, but to begin a new life from now on.

PATIENT GOWRI DEVI’S STORY: ‘STOIC COURAGE AND SWAMI’S MERCY’

Patient Gowri Devi, 45 years old, comes from Thakurganj town in Kishanganj District, Bihar. Patient Gowri’s health issues, according to her, started when a theft happened in her house, when she was just married in 1999. She always felt breathless and fearful. She started getting chest pain also, and she went for a check-up first at Purnia and then to Patna, where her illness was diagnosed. The doctor prescribed medicines, and even though she was taking them regularly, they did not help her.

Her father found out about Swami’s Hospital, and she came here in 2003. She was admitted along with her daughter, Muskan, as her caretaker during her stay in the hospital. A PTMC was done, and a second PTMC was again done in 2015 at our Hospital. She was following the doctor's advice regularly.

Unfortunately, her husband, who was a school teacher, passed away in 2018. Even though she was also working as a school teacher, due to shock and worry about her future, she neglected her health, and she stopped taking her medication. No one from her husband’s side supported her. But her parents helped her.

She started getting chest pain again in 2023. She used to pray to all the Gods all the time. But she said she did not realise she was neglecting her health. She was admitted to our Hospital on 03/05/23 and was operated on for MVR on 04/05/23. The surgery took a long time, and the patient’s daughter was concerned and called the counsellor many times. The Counsellor's assurance and prayer helped the patient’s daughter Muskan very much, and then she followed, breathing slowly, calmed down and said she felt as if her Mother was speaking to her. As her Father was not there, she needed the emotional support which she got when she needed it. Patient’s daughter Muskan and her son Sourabh, being teenagers, felt worried and restless during the surgery, and the motherly support from the Counsellor made a huge difference, she said. Later, the patient’s daughter also kept in touch with the Counsellor whenever she felt nervous.

The Counsellor told the patient and her children how important it is to follow the discharge advice given in the Discharge Summary and have a blood check-up every month, as also the medication as advised by the doctors, the diet as per the dietician’s advice, exercise and physical activity as per the physiotherapist’s advice. All of them noted all the points carefully.

They are extremely grateful to Swami, and they are going to pray to Him every day along with the other Gods. Two years have passed, and with Swami’s Grace, Patient Gowri Devi is fine. She is strictly following the doctor's advice. Her children are studying in Kolkata, and she has the courage to stay alone in her house at Thakurganj, and she still works as a teacher.

She calls the Counsellor often, and whenever she calls, she says it is all due to Swami’s Mercy(Karuna).

Tarak and Anjali received continued counselling support during their visit to SSSIHMS and thereafter through tele-counselling sessions. The experience of seeing nine months old Tuhin grow into a young, confident, intelligent and cheerful boy evokes hope despite the future challenges that he has to face. Multiple sessions of active listening to Tarak and Anjali's concerns over the past five years, has helped the counsellor listen to not just the voice but also to the spirit of the suffering. Supporting them through tele-counselling sessions has helped them manage their concerns, one day at a time with a positive frame of mind. The couple have learnt to perceive the child not as a patient but have learnt to experience the warmth of his love, the gift of his presence and the cheer he spreads around their home.

Tuhin's sister, Sanjana who is thirteen years old adores her little brother, he is the apple of his grandmother's eyes. He is blessed to be surrounded with a loving and pious family who are secure in their faith and belief that Baba is helping them every step of the way. During his visit in 2024, Tarak was heartbroken that Tuhin was not yet fit for his next surgery. The long session involving unburdening his fear and anxiety followed by the counselling session ending in BRMC and prayers helped Tarak overcome negative feelings He spontaneously requested for Vibhuthi Prasad and was grateful to receive hundred packets of Vibhuthi. Twice a day, Anjali applies Swami's Vibhuthi on Tuhin's forehead and feels confident of her child being protected. Swami's Grace and team work at SSSIHMS has provided this family with hope and acceptance. They have learnt to rise above from a state of desperation and suffering and look within at their source of strength as they experience the power of prayers, trust, faith and hope. Currently Tuhin has been enrolled in a school and mother Anjali takes him on her bicycle every morning. He enjoys the company of his classmates and plays with his friends at school, and at home in their farm, with his sister Sanjana, his grandmother and mother.

Reflecting on the meaningful moments of interacting with Tuhin's parents over the years, as a counsellor has been a great learning experience - how an innocent infant with cardiac illness can be like a catalyst empowering the parents, and providing a sense of purpose, build resilience, empowering both his parents and strengthening their faith in God and Divine intervention.
