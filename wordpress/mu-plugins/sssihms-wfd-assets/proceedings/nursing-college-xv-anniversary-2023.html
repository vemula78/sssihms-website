College of Nursing and Allied Health Sciences

Divine Message Swami willed that there should be a sufficient number of trained nursing personnel and therefore training facilities for nursing personnel will be established. It is easy for doctors to perform operations. The real difficulty is in taking care of the patients after the operations. It is easy to slice vegetables. But, to prepare a tasty dish out of that is very difficult. This task of “cooking” is done by the nurses. If there is no good nursing, even the operation may be a failure. Therefore in order to train a good group of nursing personnel, training facilities are being provided. All those who are pure-hearted and filled with a spirit of service, may approach Director for undergoing training to serve in the hospital. They need not incur any expense. The entire training is free. Elsewhere, if people want to undergo such training, they have to pay all kinds of fees. Here nothing will be charged. Everything will be provided free. Only, we want persons with pure hearts to join the course. Today we have quite a large number of people working in our hospital. But, there is more work to be done because of the ever-growing number of patients seeking relief. Hence, we need many more nursing personnel. We are asking for nurses not for Swami’s sake but for the sake of the nation.

Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, Poornachandra Auditorium, Guru Purnima message, 14 Jul 1992

College of Nursing “Hands that Help are Holier than Lips that pray”- Baba If you have compassion, caring & loving nature, patience, ability to listen to others and empathy for those who suffer, then welcome to the world of Nursing. The outcome of a successful procedure/operation depends upon the quality of care given by the nurses. Thus there is a huge requirement of skilled manpower in this field. However, acute shortage of skilled nurses and a high rate of attrition among this cadre is affecting the quality of care in Healthcare Institutions. Moreover, commercialization of Nursing education and lack of adequate training facilities has only lead to inept manpower seeking commercial gains over professional competence. As a model for ideal nursing education, Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba Instituted the Sri

Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences, College of Nursing on 1st September 2008. It is one and only College which provides Graduate Nursing education of high standards to deserving and eligible candidates completely Free of Cost. SSSIHMS, College of Nursing follows a unique concept of education called the ‘Value-Based Integrated Education’ wherein opportunities are provided to the students for an all- round development of personality. It is the only College where students go through an ‘Education in Human Values’ program which imparts value based education. The Institute aims to produce ‘Humane Healthcare Professionals’ who value patient care more than commercial gain, and who are equipped with the best of skills required to guarantee best clinical outcomes. Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences – College of Nursing is affiliated to The Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences, Bangalore for its B.Sc. Nursing program with a permitted intake of 40 seats. Admissions for the 4 year program in B.Sc. Nursing are carried out in accordance with the guidelines issued by the University from time to time.

College of Allied Health Sciences Allied Health professionals are an integral part of the medical team along with Physicians and Nurses contributing their expertise to the preventive, curative and rehabilitative care of the patients. Acute shortage of skilled professionals in these positions is affecting the quality of care in Healthcare Institutions. Moreover, lack of adequate training facilities has only lead to inexperienced manpower who pursue commercial gain over professional competence. Against this backdrop, Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba Instituted the Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences, College of Allied Health Sciences on 1st September 2008. It is one and only College which provides Graduate Allied Health Sciences education of high standards to deserving and eligible candidates completely Free of Cost. SSSIHMS, College of Allied Health Sciences follows a unique concept of education called the ‘Value-Based Integrated Education’ wherein opportunities are provided to the students for an all-round development of personality. It is the only College where students go through an ‘Education in Human Values’ program which imparts value based education. The Institute aims to produce ‘Humane Healthcare Professionals’ who value patient care more than money, and who are equipped with best of skills required to be the best in their fields.

Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences – College of Allied Health Sciences is affiliated to The Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences, Bangalore for running B.Sc. Anaesthesia Technology, B.Sc. Cardiac Care Technology, B.Sc. Imaging Technology, B.Sc. Medical Laboratory Technology and B.Sc. Perfusion Technology courses with an annual intake of 10 students in each program. Admissions for the 3 ½ year program in B.Sc. Allied Health Sciences are carried out in accordance with the guidelines issued by the University from time to time.

Dr. D. C. Sundaresh, Director, SSSIHMS

AUM SRI SAI RAM This is the fifteenth year since the Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences – College of Nursing and Allied Health Sciences was established under the divine guidance and blessings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba on 1st September, 2008. The college affiliated to the Rajiv Gandhi University, Bangalore, is renowned for providing free education along with well-furnished hostel facilities. Over the years, eleven batches of students have successfully completed their courses, with five nursing students and three allied health sciences students being awarded as subject-wise rank holders and state rank holders respectively from RGUHS. Additionally, students are honored with the Smt. Eswaramma Gold Medal for academic excellence and all-rounder gold medals each year.

The college’s founding purpose goes beyond merely conferring degrees upon students. Its primary mission is to help them cultivate self-knowledge and self-confidence, instilling in them the values of self-sacrifice and self-realization. In line with the teachings of Sri Sathya Sai University, the college follows a unique curriculum, the SSSEHV PROGRAM (Sri Sathya Sai Education in Human Values), a seven-semester course that includes lessons on important personalities, Bhagavad Gita, Bhagavad Vahini, Ramakatha Rasavahini, yoga, and more. This holistic approach aims to foster inner transformation alongside academic education, preparing students to lead fulfilling lives.

Throughout the academic year, the college organizes various events to enrich students’ experiences and promote their overall development. These events include “Sai Samskruthi Vaibhavam”, “World Health Day Celebrations”, “Gita Jayanti”, and “Gratitude Day”. As the college proudly marks its Crystal Anniversary, commemorating 15 years of dedicated service to society, it celebrates the countless individuals who have graduated with hearts filled with love and compassion and hands willing to serve fellow beings without any distinction of caste or creed.

Throughout this bygone year, the college maintained a well-structured events calendar, featuring activities like Logo and Banner Inauguration, Language Board, Seva Sathi at SSSIHMS, Vegetable Garden Activity beside Sai Karuna hostel, Quiz competition for Staff Nurses, Sevadal Activity, and more.

The Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences – College of Nursing and Allied Health Sciences stands as a shining example of the transformative power of education with its focus on nurturing not only skilled professionals but also compassionate and selfless individuals committed to serving humanity. Under the benevolent guidance of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, the college continues to inspire and empower generations of students to lead purposeful lives, imbued with the values of love, truth, and service.

Dr D C Sundaresh
Director-SSSIHMS

SSSIHMS College of Nursing and Allied Health Sciences — Beginning

Mr. Praveen Vemula

Introduction

With Divine Blessings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, the Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences, College of Nursing and Allied Health Sciences came into existence on 1st September 2008.

Divine Message

“Swami willed that there should be a sufficient number of trained nursing personnel and therefore training facilities for nursing personnel will be established. It is easy for doctors to perform operations. The real difficulty is in taking care of the patients after the operations. It is easy to slice vegetables. But, to prepare a tasty dish out of that is very difficult. This task of “cooking” is done by the nurses. If there is no good nursing, even the operation may be a failure. Therefore in order to train a good group of nursing personnel, training facilities are being provided. All those who are pure-hearted and filled with a spirit of service, may approach the Director for undergoing training to serve in the hospital. They need not incur any expense. The entire training is free. Elsewhere, if people want to undergo such training, they have to pay all kinds of fees. Here nothing will be charged. Everything will be provided free. Only, we want persons with pure hearts to join the course. Today we have quite a large number of people working in our hospital. But, there is more work to be done because of the ever-growing number of patients seeking relief. Hence, we need many more nursing personnel. We are asking for nurses not for Swami’s sake but for the sake of the nation.”

Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, Poornachandra Auditorium, Guru Pournima message, 14 Jul 1992

14 Jul 92 Gurupurnima_Nursing

Beginning I was fortunate to hear the above Divine Discourse directly from Bhagawan as a school student and these words got imprinted in my subconscious mind. Several years passed and I was fortunate to be chosen by Bhagawan to train in Hospital administration at Loma Linda University, California and Marian Medical Center, California in the year 2001-02. I got the greatest good fortune of being accepted as a staff of the Holy Temples of Healing, Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences, Prashanti Gram on 18th September 2002 and was transferred to SSSIHMS, Whitefield in March 2003. In the year 2006, I was closing the Director’s office to go for lunch and went back inside to pick up some stuff I forgot. At this time, on the World space radio which was being played on the hospital PA system, I heard the exact same portion of Swami’s talk given on 14 July 1992 and I felt as if Swami was talking to me. I spoke to Late Dr. AN Safaya, Director of Hospital in the afternoon about this discourse of Bhagawan. Dr. Safaya said that for some reason known to Bhagawan, the nursing course did not start even though efforts were made many times. I am too old now, if you want you can make an effort.

With his permission, I approached the Vice Chancellor and the Registrar of Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Learning. I was given to understand that we would need to have a faculty of medicine to start any medical related courses. Subsequently, Bhagawan made Dr. A.S. Hegde, Chief of Neurosurgery as Director. Dr. Hegde guided me to talk to somebody in the registrar office of Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences, Bangalore. They gave the process to be followed to apply to the University.

In September 2006 during Sai Sannidhi Day, I had the good fortune to talk to Bhagawan and in that interaction, I prayed to Bhagawan to give permission to start paramedical and nursing courses in our hospital. Bhagawan said “Tapaka Chesukondi” meaning “Do it without fail”. Subsequently, Dr. Hegde visited Parthi to take Swami’s blessing. Swami was very happy and told us that we should find a good principal and start the courses. We immediately applied to M/s Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences for BSc courses in Nursing, Cardiac Care Technology, Perfusion Technology, Anesthesia Technology, Imaging Technology and Medical Laboratory Technology. Since we were far behind schedule in applying, we made a special request to Dr. S.Ramanand Shetty, Vice Chancellor and Dr. Vasanth Kumar S, Registrar. They were thrilled that a completely free, value based nursing and allied health college will be coming under their purview. They made an exception and called for a special meeting of the syndicate/academic council of RGUHS and approved the proposal. As a special gesture from their side, the syndicate resolved that the Institute need not pay affiliation fees permanently.

Next, we needed to get permission from the Karnataka Government, Medical Education department. Swami always puts the right people at the right time and right place. Sri Shankar Linge Gowda, was Secretary, Medical Education. He was one of the key people involved in praying to Bhagawan to open a super speciality hospital along with Late Sri J H Patel, Chief Minister of Karnataka. We, Mohan Das sir and myself reached his office at 2:30 PM and by 3:30 PM we came out with the letter of approval. He was very thrilled at the humanitarian work being done by the hospital and also he was thrilled that a completely free nursing and allied health college, first of its kind, was going to be started in Karnataka.

With Swami’s blessings, we put a notification in the Sanathana Sarathi. Two candidates applied, Mrs. A R Manjuri and Mrs. Roopa Devi. Recruitment Committee interviewed them and forwarded to Trust for approval. We went to Swami with approval copies and photos of selected candidates. Swami was very happy that we could finish the process so fast. He blessed the photographs of Mrs. Manjuri and Mrs. Rupa Devi and Mrs. Manjuri was nominated as the principal. That evening during darshan, Swami told one of the devotees, Mrs. Sheela Nayak that we are starting a nursing college in Karnataka and not

Andhra Pradesh. Mrs. Nayak was involved in earlier attempts to start the nursing course and had several interviews from Bhagawan in the process. She was later blessed with an opportunity to participate in the construction of the Nursing College hostel and now the college building.

With Bhagawan’s blessings approval from Karnataka Nursing Council and Indian Nursing Council was also received.

We put a notification in Sanathana Sarathi inviting applications from interested candidates and conducted an entrance exam to select the students in May 2008. The lists of selected students along with their photographs were specially blessed by Swami. He went through each and every photograph of students selected and blessed them. In the month of June 2008, the classes started. Doctors, managers pitched in to teach the students initially till Nursing college got its full complement of staff.

With Bhagawan’s blessings the college was formally inaugurated on 4th October 2008 by Sri Shankar Linge Gowda, Secretary, Medical Education, Dr. S. Ramanand Shetty, Vice Chancellor, RGUHS and Dr. Vasanth Kumar S, Registrar, RGUHS.

Subsequently, Swami specifically sent a few of the elders to start the Education in Human Values courses for these students so that they learn the eternal values of our blessed land Bharat. The EHV courses were adopted from Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Learning and contained lessons on unity of religions and timeless teaching of Bhagawan.

Out of His immense love for the students, Swami allowed them to participate in the sports and cultural meet of Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Learning on 11th January and gave a wonderful trophy for their participation.

Swami continues to bless the nursing and Allied health sciences with wonderful students and teachers.

A College with a Difference

A Puppet in the Divine Hands

Ms. S. Vallishri

I was a girl with a strong-willed nature, refusing to listen to anyone and doing as I pleased. Prior to my introduction to Swami, my knowledge of spiritual figures was limited to Shirdi Sai. When my husband first showed me a picture of Sri Sathya Sai Baba, I struggled to believe or accept him as a divine being.

At the time of my marriage in March 2016, I was completing my first year of B.Sc. in Clinical Lab Technology in Coimbatore. Due to my husband’s employment at SSSIHMS-Whitefield and the availability of a similar course there, I found myself compelled to apply for admission. Admittedly, I had little interest in joining the College of Nursing and Allied Health Sciences at SSSIHMS, Whitefield. However, due to the circumstances, I had no choice but to appear for the entrance exam in June 2016.

My first encounter with Swami happened virtually when I saw His photograph and said, “If you’re truly God, then I should NOT pass this entrance exam.” I made up my mind to intentionally mark incorrect answers for the questions I knew the correct answers to, ensuring my failure so that I could return to Coimbatore and spend two more years with my parents. However, an unexpected turn of events unfolded. To play a game with me, Swami orchestrated the situation. When the results of the entrance exam were announced, my name was not on the list. Thrilled by this outcome, I began my journey back to Coimbatore. However, a few days later, I received a call from the college, asking if I would still be interested in joining the course. I was shocked and puzzled, wondering how this could happen when my name was not even shortlisted. It was undoubtedly the unseen hands of the Lord at work, ensuring that my name was included in the final list, making me a student of the ninth batch of this esteemed institution.

Upon joining the college and hostel, I encountered numerous challenges during the initial days. One of the first hurdles was the need to communicate effectively in English, a language I wasn’t proficient in. I was comfortable conversing solely in my mother tongue, Tamil. Perhaps to ease my discomfort, Swami blessed me with a wonderful group of Tamil-speaking friends, and gradually, I adjusted to the new environment and culture. During my first visit to Puttaparthi in the first year of the course, I made a plea to Swami, saying, “If you perform a miracle, then I will accept you as God.” To my astonishment, Swami orchestrated an incident that I personally experienced and will cherish forever. Vidya in Vaidyaalaya

Mr. Sai Kiran Javvaji

It is oft quoted - Imparting Health & education are the noblest of all professions. Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba made these professions ‘divine’ in His institutions by inculcating the aspects of service and spirituality to the practice of these professions. Traditionally, the ‘nobility’ of ‘health’ & ‘education’ lay in the ‘attitude of service’ with which these were to be practiced. The ‘Guru’ & the ‘Vaidya’ imparted education & health respectively, not in any expectation of a return, leave alone livelihood. However, with times, the outlook of man has changed to seeing ‘personal benefit’ in anything that he does rather than the outlook of service or contributing to society.

Even these ‘noble’ professions came to be seen as ‘lucrative’ careers & businesses and not as service to society.

The game-changer that Bhagawan is, He built institutions with completely different mottos as compared to the ‘professional’ institutions of health & education.

His dicta was – it is the society’s responsibility to cater to the health & education needs of individuals and therefore they should be provided free of cost. He took ‘money’ out of the ‘equation’ – from the point of view of the beneficiaries.

Swami said that money and education, affordability and health care delivery should not be linked. And with His institutions, whose combined life spans cross a century, He has demonstrated a practical working model – not a single rupee is charged for imparting health and education in Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Learning and Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences. In His own words, an Acharya is one who puts into practice and then preaches; and Swami no doubt, is not only an Acharya, He is the Guru of Gurus (Gurunaam Guruhu) And the blessings are not limited to the recipient patients & students; even the health care providers and the teachers are also the blessed beneficiaries of Sai institutions. Not only do they get a platform to practise their professions in their truest noblest forms, they also have an opportunity to spiritualize their lives – truly, they get the double benefit of iha & para.

In the year 2008, Swami gave us the special gift of combining health & education by opening the college of Nursing & Allied Health Sciences which is dedicated to imparting of health care education. Personally, for me, it was a lovely blessing. When I was pursuing my education in the Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Learning, I had one thought – having come to Swami, I would not go back. I would like to stay here even after my education. And seeing my teachers in the college, I was considering ‘teaching Science & Mathematics’ as one of possible ways Swami could keep me at His institutions – as if I could decide or provide options to The Omniscient and Omnipotent One. When Swami gave me the opportunity to work at the hospital, I took it up gratefully, whole-heartedly. But when the college opened in 2008, He satisfied my thought of being a teacher.

The students of this college get the perfect blend of the right attitude and skill-set. They can be the apt messengers of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba and bring back & live the ‘nobility’ that healthcare is supposed to be.
