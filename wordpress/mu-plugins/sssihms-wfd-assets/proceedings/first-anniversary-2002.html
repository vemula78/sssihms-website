First Anniversary of the Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences, Whitefield

The Union Minister for Health and the Chief Ministers of Karnataka and Maharashtra attended. Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, Prof. B.M. Hegde and other guests addressed the Health Meet.

Welcome address

Sri Y. Aravind

Department of Cardiothoracic and Vascular Surgery, SSSIHMS

▶ 11:44

As preserved in the anniversary film.

Brothers and sisters, distinguished guests, revered elders — Sai Ram to you all.

What greater gift can there be to a man than to gift him his life? To this end, our beloved Lord has gifted to us two gifts. The first: the Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences at Prasanthi Nilayam, ten years ago. And today is the first anniversary of His second gift to mankind — this edifice of medicine, this temple of healing, the Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Medical Sciences, Whitefield.

The most important thing for any human being in this world is health. ‘Health is wealth’ and ‘a healthy mind in a healthy body’ are age-old adages. The Lord, in all His mercy, has conferred upon us this opportunity of being part of His mission to serve humanity, as part of His health mission.

Let us look once more at our mission in the Lord’s mission. Any great thing that has happened to humanity has always begun as a single thought in someone’s mind. And it is a Sai sankalpa that free health care should be provided to every single person who approaches.

This reminds me of a wonderful quatrain from a qawwali. [He sings the quatrain.] It means, roughly translated: the lap of the poor, the needy and the sick is never devoid of Your grace and love, O Lord. It is Your grace, it is Your action, O Lord, that whoever comes to Your doorstep — and not only whoever comes to Your doorstep — You fill the laps of thousands and thousands of others. In what way do I glorify this wonderful action of Yours, O Lord?

Report

Report on the first year

Director, SSSIHMS

▶ 14:26

Excerpt, as preserved in the anniversary film.

My most humble, loving pranams at the Divine Lotus Feet of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, with whose bountiful grace and anugraha we are all able to celebrate the Sai Health Meet of 2002, which coincides with the first anniversary of this Institute.

Today, this super speciality hospital has become one year old in the service of humanity. The hospital has performed 1,800 heart surgeries and 1,100 brain and spinal cord surgeries in just the course of one year. [Applause] We have conducted 2,100 cardiac catheterisations in our ultra-modern cardiac catheterisation laboratory, and 150 neuro-catheterisations, both therapeutic and diagnostic. The Department of Imaging and Radiology has conducted 4,000 magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) studies and 3,900 CT scans and other complex X-ray imaging procedures during the period under review.

Ladies and gentlemen, all this work that I have enumerated has been given by the hospital completely free of charge. [Applause]

This hospital may not perform heart transplantation. This hospital may not perform brain transplantation. But it does perform heart transformation and brain transformation. [Applause] Sometimes I also say, for the sake of saying: we may duplicate this hospital; other states may develop such a hospital. But where shall we get Baba to be in the centre of it? [Applause]

Address

Dr. C.P. Thakur

Union Minister for Health, Government of India

▶ 17:18

Excerpt, as preserved in the anniversary film.

Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba; Honourable Chief Minister of Karnataka, Sri S.M. Krishna ji; Honourable Chief Minister of Maharashtra, Sri Deshmukh ji; all distinguished persons sitting on the dais; all believers in the faith of Sai Baba, all devotees, all friends, ladies and gentlemen.

I could not come last year at the time of the inauguration of this hospital; I was busy with some work. So this year I had it in my mind that I must visit this hospital, for a few reasons. The first was that I would do pranam to Sai Baba. Secondly, to see how this hospital is running without taking anything from anybody. And thirdly — you will be surprised — I was told by Singhal ji, who is associated with it, that this hospital became ready within one year. Within one year! I was surprised. And then I started quoting it in all my work in the Health Ministry, Government of India: that it should not be a ten-year programme, it should be a one-year programme. [Applause]

Dr. Safaya mentioned recognising this centre for the DNB, the Diplomate of National Board. When I assumed charge, I told the National Board the same principle — dispersal: dispersal of good medical doctors to different parts of the country. So I said, suppose this hospital is very good; depute somebody, if possible from backward areas — of Karnataka, of Kerala, of Tamil Nadu, of the North-East, of Bihar, of different parts — so that after getting training here, some of them … If they are more and more trained, they will go towards their own village side, in different parts of Karnataka, or Tamil Nadu, or the North-East, or Bihar. So dispersal is another thing. …
