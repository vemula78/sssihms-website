## Lab & Blood Bank (/lab-blood-bank/)
Laboratory complex: Pathology, Microbiology, Biochemistry labs + in-house Blood Bank. Serves cardiac & neuro specialties + SSSGH. Quality Assurance programs, full LIS, equipment interfaces, barcoding. Blood donation info page exists.

## Telemedicine (/telemedicine/)
Est. 2007. Part of Sri Sathya Sai Tele-health Network (Prasanthigram + Whitefield). Specialist centre serving 2 nodal centres: Barrackpore, West Bengal (neuro Thu & Sat, cardio Sat) and Bhubaneswar, Odisha (neuro Sat, cardio Fri). Benefits: streamlined revisit flow, appointment scheduling, fewer unnecessary journeys, post-op follow-up, counselling, online CMEs & DNB training.

## Physiotherapy (/physiotherapy/)
Inpatient-only. Electrotherapy (IFT, ultrasound, shortwave/microwave diathermy, TENS), exercise therapy, ICU physiotherapy (chest + limb), musculoskeletal, cardiopulmonary, neurological, integumentary; moist heat, cryotherapy, wax bath, therapeutic massage.

## Counseling (/counseling/) — APPLIED to services page
First hospital in India with full spirituality-based counselling dept; 40,000+ patients since 2001; pre/post-surgery + discharge; care-givers too; Carl Rogers person-centred + Sai values; presented at Mayo Clinic & Duke.

## Go Green (/gogreen/) — APPLIED
Solar: first hospital in country w/ own 100 KVA plant, +180 KVA planned. Trees: 108 transplanted (Metro works). Power: AC optimisation + LED. Water: own treatment plant (one of first in Bangalore), recycled sullage→sewage→flushing→gardens. Waste: segregated & recycled.

## DNB (/academics/dnb/) — APPLIED
NBE-accredited, free of cost, DNB = MCh/DM (super) / MD (broad). 3 gold medalists (Cardiology + CTVS). Seats: Anaesthesiology 2 (3yr) + 2 post-Diploma (2yr); Cardiology 2 (3yr, MD/DNB GenMed/Peds + SS CET); CTVS 1 (6yr); Neurosurgery 1 (6yr). Admission only via NBE centralised counselling.
